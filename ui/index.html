Shared gear. Clear responsibilities.

AKS is an intermediary connecting independent equipment owners and renters. Rental agreements are directly between those parties. AKS does not own, inspect or insure listed equipment and does not guarantee its condition or any transaction.

To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, AKS accepts no responsibility or liability for loss, theft, damage, injury, non-return or disputes arising from a rental. Nothing in this notice excludes liability or rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

Arrange coverage before pickup. Renters are strongly encouraged to purchase suitable rented-equipment insurance. Owners should request a current certificate of insurance (COI) and confirm coverage with the insurer or broker, including equipment replacement value, rental dates, theft, damage, transit, exclusions and deductibles. A COI is evidence of insurance; it does not create or extend coverage. AKS provides no insurance, damage waiver or coverage verification.
